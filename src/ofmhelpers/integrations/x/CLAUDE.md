# x

Logged-out read of a public X profile for /accounts (`socials/platforms/x.py`). Free, no login.

- `client.py` `XClient(proxy).profile(username)`: GET `https://x.com/<user>` through `outbound.page_session(settings.x, proxy)` (`OFM_X_PROXIES`, `_TIMEOUT_S`, `_HTTP_RETRIES`). 404 = `XNotFoundError`, 429 = `XRateLimitedError`, other 4xx/5xx = `XUnavailableError`, no profile in the page = `XError` ("suspended, or X changed its page").
- Page (verified 2026-11-21 on NASA, jack, DanewalkerJ): a TanStack/seroval page; X's own GraphQL answers sit in it as JS string literals `kind:"Result.Ok",value:"<JSON>"`. `embedded_answers` turns each JS literal into JSON (`\xNN` and other JS-only escapes rewritten, `\uXXXX` pairs left to `json`) and keeps the ones that parse. `UserByScreenName`: `rest_id`, `core.screen_name/name`, `relationship_counts.followers/following`, `privacy.protected`, `avatar.image_url` (`_normal` -> `_400x400`), `verification.is_blue_verified`. `UserOriginalsTimeline`: own posts only (no reposts, no replies, the pinned one in its own instruction is not read), each `rest_id`, `details.created_at_ms` (else the snowflake time), `views.count`, `media_entities2[].media_url_https`; a `TweetWithVisibilityResults` wrapper is unwrapped (`tweet`).
- Logged out X shows at most `LOGGED_OUT_POSTS` (5) own posts, then `TimelineTerminateTimeline`: no paging. It also leaves posts out: @DanewalkerJ 2026-11-21 had `tweet_counts.tweets` 8, the timeline gave 4 (176k views of the owner's ~500k; which 4 are hidden not checked, theory: sensitive media). So a logged-out read is never `complete`: the scan reaches back to its oldest shown post, and a window older than what consecutive scans covered reads as unknown (socials/CLAUDE.md `covered_since`). The hidden posts are missing from every window: full numbers need a logged-in read or Apify (below).
- This page read is the second source; Apify reads X first (`socials/platforms/x_apify.py`, owner 2026-11-21: "use apify, free tier gives $5"). Test runs that day on @DanewalkerJ, every actor saw all 8 posts: `kaitoeasyapi/twitter-x-data-tweet-scraper-pay-per-result-cheapest` $0.00375 per account (wired), `apidojo/twitter-scraper-lite` $0.019 per account (too dear for $5 a month), `apidojo/tweet-scraper` $0 in free-plan demo mode (max 10 items per run: unusable for batches).
- `fake.py`: `x.com` under OFM_FAKE_EXTERNALS: `fake_x` (3 posts, a `'` sent as `\x27` like the real page), anything else 404. Contract: `tests/contract/test_x_contract.py` (real half under `live`: nasa).
