This policy explains what Ascend Global ("we") collects through ascendglobal.cash, why, and the choices you have. It covers the public website, its application form, and the internal content tools our team signs in to.

What we collect

  • Application form. The name, email, phone number, and Telegram or Instagram handle you enter, to review your application and contact you about it.
  • Team tools. The files our team uploads or generates (images and videos) and a record of the jobs run on them, so the tools can show and reuse them.
  • Session cookie. One cookie that keeps a signed-in team member signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs. IP address and requested pages, kept for security and troubleshooting.

Google user data

Team members can choose to connect a Google account to upload finished videos to their own Google Drive. When you do:

  • What we access: only the drive.file permission — files and folders this app creates in your Drive. We cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. We also read your Google account's email address to show which account is connected.
  • How we use it: solely to create one folder and upload the videos you ask us to upload into it, when you click "Upload to Drive".
  • What we store: your account email and the OAuth token Google issues, on our own server, so the upload can run in the background. We do not store the contents of your Drive.
  • Sharing: we never sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person at Ascend Global reads it, except where needed for security or to comply with the law.
  • Deletion: the stored token is deleted when you sign out, or when Google reports it revoked. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in your Drive, under your control.

Ascend Global's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

We use hosting (our server) and, for the team's AI generation tools, kie.ai, which receives the reference media a team member chooses to submit. Google user data is never sent to them.

Retention

Applications are kept until reviewed and then deleted on request. Team files are kept while the team uses them. Drive tokens are deleted as described above.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, using the contact below. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
