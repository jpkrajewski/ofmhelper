Ascend Global LLC ("Ascend Global", "we", "us") runs ascendglobal.cash: a public website where creators apply to work with us, and Content Ops, a private tool our team and invited partners sign in to. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to have it deleted.

1. What we collect

  • Application form. What you type: name, email, phone, Telegram and Instagram handles, revenue range, experience and goals.
  • Signing in. With Google or Apple: your account's ID, email address and name. With a password: your email and a one-way hash of the password (scrypt), never the password itself.
  • Google Drive. Only if you connect it. See section 2.
  • What you add to Content Ops. Images and videos you upload, social media handles you track, phone and login records, provider API keys and cookie files. Passwords, keys, cookies and tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Technical data. IP address, browser details and request logs, used to keep the service secure (rate limits, sign-in protection) and to fix errors. A session cookie keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. Google user data

Content Ops works inside one folder of your Drive, named "ALL ACCOUNTS", which it creates (or finds, if you already have it). With your permission it:

  • creates and names folders inside it: one per social media account you manage, and folders such as POSTED for videos already posted;
  • lists the files in those folders and reads their names, dates and thumbnails, to show what is waiting to be posted and what was posted;
  • downloads videos from those folders when you ask for edited copies of them;
  • uploads the videos you choose into those folders;
  • shares an account's folder with the Google account of the person who manages that account in Content Ops;
  • moves files it uploaded to your Drive trash when you remove them in Content Ops;
  • reads how much Drive storage is free, to warn you before it runs out.

It does not open, change or delete files outside that folder. Full Drive access is needed because the videos are put into these folders from phones with the Google Drive app: a narrower scope would only show files Content Ops itself created.

Ascend Global's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • we use Google user data only to provide the features you see and ask for in Content Ops;
  • we do not sell it, and do not use or transfer it for advertising, credit checks or to data brokers;
  • we transfer it to others only as needed to provide those features (for example, a video you choose to send to an AI provider to generate or caption it), for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets after asking for your explicit consent first;
  • no person at Ascend Global reads it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it is needed for security or abuse investigations, or the law requires it;
  • we do not use it to train or improve artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

The access token Google gives us is stored encrypted on our server. Copies of videos downloaded from your Drive are kept only as long as needed for the edits you ask for, or until you ask us to delete your data.

3. How we use data

  • to run Content Ops and the features you use;
  • to answer applications and contact applicants about them;
  • to keep accounts and the service secure, and to prevent abuse;
  • to find and fix errors and slow pages.

4. Who we share data with

Only service providers that process data for us, to run the features above:

  • server hosting (OVHcloud);
  • error and performance monitoring (Pydantic Logfire), which receives request details including the signed-in account's email;
  • AI generation and captioning providers, only for the images, videos and prompts you send to them (for example kie.ai, Higgsfield, RunningHub, Elyum, APIYI, Google Gemini, Groq);
  • services that read public social media statistics for accounts you track (for example Apify);
  • Google and Apple, for signing in, and Google, for Drive;
  • Telegram, for notifications sent to our own team.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time: Account → Unlink Google. The stored token is deleted at once. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your account and data: ask us (section 9). We delete it within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active; applications until you ask us to delete them; monitoring records are deleted automatically after a limited period.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed; tokens, keys, cookies and saved passwords are encrypted at rest. Every person sees only their own data, and every request is checked against the signed-in account.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Ask us (section 9); we answer within 30 days.

8. Age and changes

The site and Content Ops are for adults (18+) only. If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
