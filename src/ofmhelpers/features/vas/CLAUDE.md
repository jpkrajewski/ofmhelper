# features/vas

VAs (virtual assistants), sessions, View as, managers and the owner's backlog notes of 2026-11. Moved here from `web/CLAUDE.md`; the access map and the `Viewer` themselves are in `auth/access.py` and `features/users/viewing.py`.

## Sessions and View as (2026-11-14)
- **The session slides.** Starlette re-sends the cookie only when the session is written, and nothing wrote it after sign-in: it ended 5 h after sign-in whatever the person did (owner: "user is getting logged out when using the app"). `AuthMiddleware` now writes `seen` on every user action (a page navigation, any non-read method), so the cookie lives `session_max_age_s` (5 h) from the last action; a script's GET poll does not count. `session.js` sends `PUT /session` (204) on a click or key press, at most once a minute, and moves its own deadline on the answer. No hard maximum on purpose (owner: 5 h, renewed by every action). The cookie is the whole session (signed, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, `Secure` when `SESSION_HTTPS_ONLY`), so a copy of it works while it is used.
- **Logout ends every session of the person** (owner 2026-11-14; before, a copy of the cookie outlived Logout). `users.sessions_ended_at` is set by Logout (`features/signin/outcome.signed_out`); a session carries when it signed in (`since`) and the middleware refuses one from before that moment ("logged out"), on every device. A password change ends the others too (`credential_stamp`). Sessions from before the deploy have no `since`: they go at their person's first Logout.
- **View as an own VA acts.** `Viewer.acts`: the admin viewing as a VA whose `owner_id` is the admin may do what the VA may (the run is the VA's, on the admin's keys). Anyone else stays look only: 403 "You are viewing as <name>. Go back to your view to do this." Phone passwords and the export are never shown under View as (`web/routers/inventory/CLAUDE.md`).
- **A page that is not theirs goes home (2026-11-14).** `AuthMiddleware.require`: a GET page load (`sec-fetch-mode: navigate`) of an area the role may not open (a VA on /accounts, anyone on a retired page) answers 303 to /home, not `{"detail":"Not available to you"}`; a fetch and every write keep the 403. The same for an address no route matches (`web/exceptions.py` `http_error`); a route's own 404 stays.
- A card of a VA's job shows the owner "Done by VA: <name>" on its own line under the meta line (`_gallery_card.html`, `[data-made-by]`; generation.js `keepMaker` carries it onto the card redrawn when the run ends).

## VA control (2026-11-14, owner's backlog)
- **A VA's access** is the map in `auth/access.py`: no /account (the page load goes home; base.html loads `drive-alert.js` and its banners only for who has /account, and shows a VA's email as text). One page under "More" (a VA's Edit) is a plain bar link.
- **A VA hides nothing** (`Action.HIDE`, members only; owner: "what if VA hides clones from me"). `POST /jobs/{id}/hidden` takes `Actors`: the owner hides and unhides their own cards and their VAs'; a VA gets 403, has no Hide / Unhide / "Show hidden", and their gallery ignores `hidden_at` (a card the owner hid stays in the VA's). A card rendered outside a page is handed `request` (/clone/run); without one a card offers Hide (unit renders).
- **A team shares its subjects.** `Viewer.team_ids` = the owner and all their VAs, the same for each of them; /clone's Last used / Most used tiles are the team's runs counted together (`subject_picks(viewer.team, ...)`, `ParamHistory.actor` one person or a set), so a VA sees exactly the owner's tiles. The Reuse browser's "last picked" list is keyed by the store's owner (`features/assets/ref_usage.py`): one list for the owner and their VAs. `user_ids` (whose jobs a gallery shows) is unchanged: a VA still sees only their own. The provider a form starts on stays the person's own.
- **Gallery filters** (toolbar of /generate, /clone, /download-assets): `?va=<id>` (the reader or one of their VAs, anyone else 404); /clone alone has the switch "Show not uploaded to Drive" (`?not_uploaded=1`, `JobRepository.page_not_uploaded`, uncached, `NOT EXISTS`; not for a VA). Both are plain links built by `GalleryQuery.link(...)`, no script; they ride in `GalleryQuery.filters` with the dates, so scroll and live poll stay inside. `/drive?upload=<job>` finds a VA's run for the owner (Upload to Drive on a VA's card opened /drive with no video before).
- **/vas**: each cell two facts only (owner 2026-11-14: "clean information"): the dollars and the number of runs (no "est.", no "without a price"; a period with no priced run shows "—"). Days are the reader's zone.
- **The time zone is the person's own** (`schemas/time_zones.py`, `static/js/time-zone.js`): the browser's zone goes into the `ofm_timezone` cookie by itself and follows them; the server takes any real IANA zone (label = its city). The globe still changes it: "Automatic" (their own again) or one of `TIME_ZONES`, a pick remembered in that browser (`localStorage` `ofm_timezone_picked`). A page printing times reloads once when it was drawn in another zone. No IP lookup.

- **Last seen and the VA's 2 hours.** `users.last_seen_at`: the middleware keeps each action on the person, at most one write a minute (`features/users/presence.py`). A session is refused ("idle") once its last action is older than its role's limit: a VA `OFM_VA_SESSION_MAX_AGE_S` 7200, everyone else the cookie's 5 h; `data-session-max-age` is the role's, so session.js counts the same. The cookie alone leaves no trace, so the sweep `va idle` (`features/users/idle.py`) writes an `idle_logout` event dated at the VA's last action and ends their sessions (`sessions_ended_at`); a sign-in settles a pending one first. Once per idle stretch; never for a user or an admin.
- **What the owner is told** (`features/vas/notices.py`, events on the bus, Telegram). The milestones are looked at when a VA's job ends (`run_job`, after the cost is kept), keyed `vas.reels:<va>:<day>:<step>` so the bus sends each step once; the day is `OFM_REPORTS_TIMEZONE`'s. Best effort: never fails the job. Limits: Telegram chats are global (every owner's VA notices land in the same chats); an unpriced run adds no dollars.

- **Guided tour** (`ui.tour`, `static/js/tour.js`; owner: "like a google walkthrough"): the steps are (CSS selector, text) pairs each page template writes next to its own controls (`{% set tour = [...] %}` before the toolbar include): no schema, a step whose control is not on the page is left out. For a VA it starts by itself on the page's first opening after every sign-in (localStorage `tour:<path>` = the session's `since`). The owner's one exception to "no help text".
- **Made-up data, development only:** `python -m ofmhelpers.features.vas.seed seed <va> | clear` (in the app container; refused under `OFM_ENV=production`). Rows only, ids `seed-...`, copied from the team's real finished jobs so cards show real files. `clear` deletes exactly those rows. Never calls a provider or the runner (so no notices).

## Backlog 2026-11-15 (owner's backlog.txt)
- **No money at a provider -> Telegram** (`features/jobs/notices.py`): key per provider per `OFM_NO_CREDITS_NOTICE_EVERY_S` (3600). Sent where a provider failure is recorded: `run_job` (`ProviderNoCreditsError`, `ApifyCreditError`, by class) and recovery (`TaskState.no_credits`, the Higgsfield "credit balance is too low" seen on prod job e564f26c).
- **/vas Hours** (`features/vas/hours.py`): a session ends at its Logout or idle logout (dated at the last action: the 2 idle hours are not worked), still open = the last action (`last_seen_at`), followed by another sign-in = its last job before it (two devices; days before idle logouts were recorded, 2026-11-14). Split at local midnight.
- **A VA's own Drive folder** (`features/drive/va_folders.py`, table `drive_va_folders`): made when the VA is added (`features/users/service.py` `_make_va_folder` queues the owner's `drive_sync`) and by every sweep (owners with VAs and no phones too), renamed with the VA. A VA's employer never changes after the add (owner 2026-11-18; `set_owner` refuses), so a VA never has a folder in a second root. /drive lists them under "VAs" by name, never among the folders no phone owns. An upload goes into an account folder (`login_id`) or a VA's (`va_id`), exactly one: CHECK `ck_drive_uploads_one_folder`, unique per file per VA; the worker sends it as the owner's Google account.
- **/handmade** (`features/handmade/service.py`, `web/routers/inventory/handmade.py`; owner: "i want to see what is in handmade by who and i choose where to upload ... let it stay on the server"): one job `handmade_upload` per reel keeps it on the server (`uploads/handmade/<dir>`), nothing goes to Drive by itself. The owner takes a VA's upload back out too (`remove_upload(..., readers)`, also on /clone).
- **/edit's timeline is the result** (owner: "it should just disappear from the timeline", "there should be reverse button"): a label dragged moves that video's clips, its x drops them, a click marks them; Cut sends the marked part: one stretch (`video/start/end`) or the clips in it (`clips`, joined on the edit worker: `features/edit/clips.py`, `media/trim.py` `part` + `join`).
- **A VA never opens Drive**: on a VA's /clone cards "In Drive: <folder>" is text, no link and no x (`can(request, "drive-upload")`; clone.js draws the link only where clone.html rendered the x template).

## Backlog 2026-11-15, second batch (owner's backlog.txt; plan `~/.claude/plans/keen-scribbling-valley.md`)
- **Manager** (`Role.MANAGER`; the proof that a role is an entry).
  - Not expressible in the map: "sees but runs nothing" and "whose data". That is `features/users/viewing.py` `_managing`: a manager's `Viewer` is their employer's (`users.owner_id`: any active user or admin, owner 2026-11-16; never a VA or a manager), with `by` = the manager, `acts=False` (look only: every write 403 "A manager looks only: nothing can be changed here."), `pages=Role.MANAGER` and `keys_of=None`.
  - Everything role-based goes through `auth.access.opens(viewer, what)`: what the role's list allows, and for a manager only what the employer may open too, so a plain user's manager has no /chatting (the chats are an admin's). Used by `AuthMiddleware.require`, `templates_config._can` and `schemas.nav.nav_of` (base.html, home.html).
  - base.html shows the manager's own email and no "Viewing as" banner, and shows the chat pop-ups to whoever can open /chatting.
  - `Action.CONNECT_CHATS` (admins) guards `/chatting/fanvue/connect|callback`: GETs that save a login, which look-only cannot stop.
  - A manager signs in by email (a set-password link) or Google, like a user. Phone passwords stay hidden (`by` is set). A manager with no employer sees only themselves (nothing).
- Telegram per owner: `integrations/senders/CLAUDE.md`. Stacked key on /account: `integrations/stacked/CLAUDE.md`. Who did what: `db/CLAUDE.md` (audit_log, row stamps, `db/acting.py`).
- **A job runs again on the fakes**: `OFM_FAKE_EXTERNALS=true python -m ofmhelpers.features.jobs.replay <job_id>` in the app container on dev.
  - It runs here, through `run_job`, never on a queue (a worker would run against real services), and is refused unless fakes are on and this is not production.
  - What runs: `RESTARTS` as restarted; `RESUMABLE` with its provider's fake key (`replay.FAKE_KEYS`); trials from `params.args`. Anything else names why (`NOT_REPLAYABLE`, the legacy per-model tools).
  - A job's input files must be on this disk: `prod_to_dev.sh` copies the database only.
- **Nothing fails silently** (`.claude/rules/code.md` Logging): not changed on purpose: a download job whose every link failed stays `done`. Its card shows each failed link with its reason (owner 2026-11-15), and the span is `error` level.
